Yarn Box helps you organize your yarn collection. Record a name, brand, color, storage location, and personal notes. Add one optional photo to recognize the yarn you need.

Search all five text fields, edit entries, and browse your collection. JPEG and PNG photos are saved with each entry. Your original image files stay unchanged.

Your collection stays on your computer. No account or internet connection is required. The interface is available in German, English, Spanish, French, Italian, and Portuguese (Brazil). Language changes take effect the next time you start the app.
